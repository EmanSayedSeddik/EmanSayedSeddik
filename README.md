### 📂 Featured Analytics Projects

#### 1️⃣ [Global Electronics – Multi-Market Analytics Dashboard](https://github.com/EmanSayedSeddik)
* **Stack:** `Power BI` `Power Query` `DAX` `Data Modeling`
* **Overview:** A 6-page enterprise analytics dashboard analyzing **$52.56M** in sales across 8 countries, product categories, store performance, and average delivery delay metrics.
* **Key Highlights:** Star schema modeling, delivery lead-time analysis, profit margin benchmarks (56.06%), and demographic segmentation.

#### 2️⃣ [Carrefour – Retail Performance & Sales Dashboard](https://github.com/EmanSayedSeddik)
* **Stack:** `Power BI` `Power Query` `Retail Analytics` `RTL Support`
* **Overview:** High-level interactive 3-page retail dashboard analyzing **$21.53M** in sales across branches (Madinaty, Cairo, Alex), departments (Groceries, Frozen), and trading days.
* **Key Highlights:** Full Arabic RTL interface support, MoM comparisons, and city-level sales distribution.

#### 3️⃣ [FIFA World Cup 2026 – Tournament Analytics](https://github.com/EmanSayedSeddik)
* **Stack:** `Power BI` `DAX` `Sports Analytics`
* **Overview:** Comprehensive 7-page report modeling the expanded 48-team tournament, tracking 104 matches, 308 goals, continental confederations, stadium specifications, and $775M prize pool distribution.

#### 4️⃣ [Jumia & E-Commerce Operations & Returns Analysis](https://github.com/EmanSayedSeddik)
* **Stack:** `Python` `Power BI` `Data Cleaning` `Operations BI`
* **Overview:** End-to-end audit of 118K+ transaction records, evaluating gross vs. net revenue, size demand, and root-cause drivers behind the 9.06% cancellation rate across fulfilment channels.
